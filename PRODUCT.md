# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Adult parents and caregivers in Philadelphia seeking food support and help with electricity, gas or water bills. They may be stressed, using a shared phone, short on time or unfamiliar with benefit systems. Plain language, accessible alternatives and dependable recovery are essential.

## Product Purpose

CaseLink takes minimum household information and one informed, bounded service authorization, then independently prepares and pursues permitted support. Parents participate when an actual fact, personal act, permission expansion or meaningful choice requires them. Routine judgments and follow-up belong to the service.

## Product Authority

The [parent PRD](docs/parent-support-mvp-prd.md) defines complete scope; the [implementation plan](docs/parent-support-implementation-plan.md) defines delivery and gates. [Progress](docs/parent-support-progress.md) distinguishes implemented code from provider and release readiness.

## Operating Context

- Public urgent food and utility routes work without an account or AI wait.
- Get support, My support and Request detail form one coherent family experience.
- Existing CaseLink email/password identities support sign-in in the reviewed shared-account mode; email codes remain available where enrollment permits. Household data, documents and background authority remain separate from school records and roles.
- Food now, SNAP new/renewal/pending/problems, relevant WIC, electricity/gas/water and combined requests remain the finished scope.
- Parents can correct facts, inspect permission and evidence, pause/cancel future work, export information and request deletion.
- One informed mandate names programs, actions, data, preferences, limits and expiry. AI cannot expand its authority.
- Agencies decide eligibility and benefits. Preparation, sending, receipt, acceptance, approval and support actually received have distinct evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use Next.js, React, Supabase, server-side GPT-6.1 Sol Responses, protected durable workers and tested documents. Privacy, provenance, idempotency, revocation, recovery and accessibility are required behavior.

The parent implementation is under verification. Live execution requires current program permissions and real verified channels; source listings and partnership alone do not enable it. Real household processing, provider rehearsals, reviewed consequential language, family observation and a release decision are separate gates. Public copy must describe actual readiness.

## Brand Commitments

CaseLink should feel composed, welcoming and capable. Reuse the original lavender/navy/violet tokens, self-hosted Newsreader and Geist, and one shared CaseLink logo. The owner explicitly selected imagegen Option 2, Family Panorama: editorial heading and action above original family imagery, with a shared food/utility entry surface. Carry the same identity into operational and policy pages, with readable controls and honest states. Preserve the linked ChatGPT Futures recognition and existing policy/contact commitments. Avoid technical agent language, unsupported promises, fabricated progress, testimonials or metrics.

## Historical Scope

The school product is preserved in [school product context](docs/legacy/school-product.md), [school design](docs/legacy/school-design.md) and the baseline branch. Its staff review workflow does not govern parent features; the original visual identity and existing accounts are deliberately reused.
