The source permits starting a filing with **name, address and signature**. It requests other answers for the eligibility decision, except where optional, but does not specify every absence alternative or validation rule. In particular it does not say a family must possess a telephone to file. A complete preparation flow must preserve valid minimum filing access while resolving genuinely required facts; no invented zero, No, date, signature or contact value may fill a gap.

The original form has **no detailed immigration questionnaire**, alien number/date/category/sponsor table, separate self-employment worksheet, general asset/vehicle/property inventory, medical dollar table, diagnosis/disability checkbox, work-exemption questionnaire, email or credentials fields. Self-employment is an income-source example. Resources appear in applicant expedited comparisons and agency-only decisions. Medical applicability text concerns a member who is 60+ or disabled, but is not itself a printed answer field. Any later interview or supplemental requirement needs its own verified recipient contract rather than being attributed to this form.

Race/ethnicity and voter choices are explicitly optional and stay omitted by default. All CAO registration, voter-administration and expedited-decision fields remain agency-only. This inventory must not turn these areas into family prompts or inferred agent answers.

The next implementation should ask known applicable missing facts together before background work, reuse permitted current evidence, prepare the complete accepted form, then present one final overview and a genuine Submit action with recorded receipt and next steps. Expanding storage, extraction or model projection for names, birthdates, immigration/history/health information requires the actual purpose, policy and protected-channel decisions first. This file authorizes none of those expansions and leaves the renderer explicitly incomplete.
