## The finished family experience

1. Start with “Let's find food support that works for your family.” Let families describe their situation or answer simple guided questions; neither path requires choosing SNAP, WIC or a pantry. Ask urgency and minimum routing information; show urgent help immediately without account creation or waiting for AI.
2. CaseLink determines which supported paths may fit: food needed now, a new SNAP application, renewal, a pending case, a routine benefits problem and relevant WIC support. Reuse permitted fresh information and ask known required questions together. Do not silently turn every food request into a new application.
3. Explain the actual proposed programs and obtain one informed, bounded mandate covering named recipients, actions, data use, duration and controls. Use applicable document/private-field notices before capture. Ask again only for expanded scope or a genuine necessary personal act; preserve the explicitly requested SNAP final review/Submit.
4. CaseLink prepares and executes supported work, reconciles uncertain delivery, follows up and continues after the page closes. A viewer inspects work already triggered by the service. Parents complete only indispensable facts, personal authentication/certification/interviews, meaningful choices or an unavoidable qualified handoff.
5. My support shows what happened, what CaseLink is doing, who acts next, any real deadline and when the next check is due. Program outcomes stay separate. A SNAP wait does not stop useful immediate-food work.
6. CaseLink handles routine permitted document requests and follow-up using existing authorized information, then records a sourced decision and whether assistance was actually received. An unresolved route remains honestly unresolved with an owned next step.

Keep the original CaseLink identity and existing forms/workers/private services. The needs-first entry improves access; it must not delay fixing actual submission/status connections behind a homepage redesign.

### Family example and stopping point
