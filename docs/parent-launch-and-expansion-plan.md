# CaseLink launch and expansion plan

Decision date: October 27, 2026 (America/New_York). Status: researched, independently reviewed delivery plan; no new route, data purpose, enrollment cohort or external action is activated by this document. Product authority remains the [PRD](parent-support-mvp-prd.md); this document supplies its release sequence, route evidence and delivery backlog.

## 1. Stable direction and release contract

Build one parent-owned service that turns a need into confirmed help, with AI handling permitted routine administration from a bounded mandate through follow-up. Keep the existing CaseLink product, stack, household controls, worker and Option 2 identity. A provider connection can fail without restarting the product or replacing its engineering foundations.

The first family MVP has two complete workflows in Philadelphia:

1. **Food access:** a confirmed allocation/pickup arrangement at a participating school/community distribution, followed through to collection or an honestly unresolved outcome. Delivery is included only if that exact route supports it.
2. **Adult employment support:** a confirmed first career-support appointment, prepared materials where relevant, cancellation/change handling and follow-up. A generic event RSVP, a resume download or a counselor callback request is not this completion contract. Appointment attendance and employment obtained remain separate outcomes.

This is an explicit staged-release decision. It supersedes the earlier requirement that every food/utility C-01 journey must be implemented before any bounded family MVP can launch. **It does not remove C-01, reduce quality/privacy gates or declare current functionality ready.** The full food/utility release follows the MVP; the remaining original catalog follows in named expansions. Public promises must identify exactly which release and supported services are available.

No essential step may be secretly completed by a CaseLink operator and counted as autonomous. Provider stock allocation, food packing/driving and the counselor's substantive service are distinct from routine CaseLink administrative work; record both. A family may still need a genuine declaration, signature, identity step, missing fact or meaningful choice. Prepare everything else and resume automatically. Never promise funding, employment, a seat or delivery before the responsible provider's evidence exists.

### Route checkpoint: three business days, not three days of promised provider response

Beginning October 27, target an owner/resource checkpoint on **October 30, 2026**. The product/resource owner supplies or authorizes obtaining the actual operating answers. No outside contact is authorized by this plan; owner-run contact or separately authorized outreach is required. Engineering and independent review inspect the evidence; they do not manufacture a partner contract.

For **each** MVP route, record:

- A named real program/operator and permitted geography, adults served, service offered and supported languages.
- Exact required fields, their source/purpose/retention, understandable permission and any genuinely required personal act. Inspect actual custom questions: employment intake can require identifiers; food intake can introduce health/child data. The first route should avoid those when the provider permits a minimal alternative.
- Who is allowed to reserve/book, how capacity is represented, what constitutes acceptance, and how to reconcile a timeout without duplicating a transaction. For food, the actual owner must authorize the allocation and its access/priority rules, including applicable supplier/funder terms; school coordination alone does not establish that authority. Do not carve stock from walk-in supply or change eligibility/priority to manufacture a booking workflow.
- An actual means of reading the known reservation/appointment, receiving changes, cancelling, handling expiry/no-show and attributing fulfillment. A sent email is not acceptance.
- A permitted controlled test, a responsible exception contact, response/stock-update expectations and a route stop mechanism.

Record each answer as **verified / provider confirmation pending / not supported**, with dated evidence. If a selected provider cannot support the contract, inspect at most one alternate within the **same workflow** before the next weekly checkpoint. A failure changes a transport/provider dependency, not the product, design or household model. If neither can support it, keep that advertised capability closed and record the exact owner dependency. Do not automatically substitute a resume generator, invent an API, remove food from the MVP or hide a manual operator. Rebaseline dates explicitly; the MVP still needs both working workflows.

## 4. MVP experience and exact evidence

1. **Choose help.** Two clear live services; food access and adult career support. Existing unsupported benefits/utilities have honest official access/help, not fake Apply controls or speculative sensitive intake.
2. **Give the necessary information once.** Reuse permitted current facts; ask known route questions together. Booking needs confirmed contact, timezone, availability/preferences and verified host questions; food needs only the selected distribution's verified minimum. Document processing remains optional unless an actual route requires evidence. Do not ask for SNAP, SSNs, pregnancy, bank access or every child's identity for these simple routes.
3. **Authorize the named service.** Explain recipients, actions, exact scope, use of information, limits/expiry and stop control. Confirm booking instructions/preferences upfront according to the actual route; add a later choice only when the provider requires it or a material unresolved choice remains. No automatic signing or invented high-confidence threshold.
4. **Watch real work.** The checklist distinguishes checking availability, request pending, confirmed reservation/booking, required action, cancelled/expired, attended/collected and unknown/unresolved. Show real date/time/location, evidence age, cancellation terms and next trigger. A browser viewer appears only for an actual owned browser route; API work displays receipts, not a simulated website.
5. **Return and continue.** Safe reminders, changes, correction and cancellation work after sign-out/page close under current authority. A missed heartbeat or stale status is uncertainty, not false success. Update the same request and preserve prior attempted/accepted versions.
6. **Record the actual result.** Booking confirmation is separate from attendance; food reservation is separate from collection. Host/provider reports and family reports retain attribution. No-show evidence does not prove attendance. A job offer, agency award or bill reduction is never inferred from these early steps.

For the first calendar route use **one-to-one appointments**. Verify current narrow provider scopes and actual custom questions. The [scope catalog](https://developer.calendly.com/docs/authentication/scopes) and [availability endpoint](https://developer.calendly.com/api-docs/calendly-api/event-types/list-event-type-available-times) differ in their scope descriptions; verify the required narrow read/write grants during the controlled connection. The [official FAQ](https://developer.calendly.com/docs/getting-started/frequently-asked-questions) explains cancellation applies to an entire event and there is no direct rescheduling API. Do not apply it to group classes. Implement cancellation plus rebooking only if the host accepts that process and its failure/reconciliation behavior is proved. Otherwise provide the genuine necessary change action and measure the remaining family effort. [Webhook subscriptions](https://developer.calendly.com/api-docs/calendly-api/webhooks/create-webhook-subscription) may expose other events: use a dedicated host or known-operation reconciliation, reject unrelated data before storage, [verify callback authenticity](https://developer.calendly.com/api-docs/overview/webhooks/webhook-signatures) and never ingest a whole organization's calendar for convenience. An OAuth development environment alone does not isolate bookings or suppress messages; use a dedicated controlled event and inboxes for tests.

## 6. What must be true to launch

Release the bounded MVP only when both live workflows satisfy their declared contracts and all applicable PRD privacy, authority, quality and operating gates pass. Account signup/recovery, stop/correct/delete, truthful receipts and accessible return use are MVP requirements, not later polish.

- Exact deployment, migrations, provider permissions/fields, model/prompts, policy versions, language coverage and working recipients are recorded. Unsupported capabilities are unavailable honestly, without changing the frozen release scope after tests.
- Relevant unit, real isolated Auth/DB/Storage, integration, headless end-to-end, accessibility/visual, adversarial and document-render checks pass. Shared authorization/worker changes retain legacy regressions. No irrelevant PDF check substitutes for a real booking/food confirmation.
- Both routes' ordinary eligible reference cases run from the informed start to verified acceptance and routine follow-up without an extra approval click or a CaseLink operator completing the task. Required personal/provider/physical service steps remain explicit; the aggregate autonomy floor below is not permission to leave a normal happy path manual.
- At least **60 reviewed synthetic multi-step cases across the two MVP routes**, all advertised languages and enabled actions, plus the partner-approved real channel rehearsals. This is the MVP corpus; full C-01 later needs its own complete coverage. Evaluate factuality, data reuse, necessary interventions, source changes, prompt injection, cost and failure recovery. Synthetic test fixtures are testing tools, never product fallbacks.
- Freeze routine task denominators before evaluation. Proposed acceptance remains at least **80% of eligible routine administrative tasks** without extra family prompts or CaseLink operator completion, with all required personal acts and physical/substantive service work reported separately. Missing connectors, abandoned/failed/unknown work and routine operator fixes remain visible; do not choose trivial tasks to inflate the score.
- Zero observed critical failures in the release evidence: unauthorized disclosure/action, invented personal fact/signature/capacity/receipt, duplicate transaction, lost correction or unowned urgent issue. A small pilot cannot establish absence of rare failures or legal compliance. Unnecessary approval loops and routine hidden operator work require repair too.
- Parents observed in the pilot understand what was done, whether it is confirmed, what they must do and how to stop/change it. Both supported route outcomes and counterexamples are represented. Publish limits and actual support hours; a confirmation is not a guaranteed job, meal composition or agency decision.
- Named product/service, engineering, privacy/security and partner/resource owners approve the exact release record, including launch/support capacity, costs, incident/restore procedures and unresolved risks. No production activation or new real-family collection occurs merely because this plan says MVP.
