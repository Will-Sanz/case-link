# Parent service privacy and regulatory review

Reviewed October 25, 2026. Scope: the direct-to-parent Pennsylvania food and utility service described in [the PRD](parent-support-mvp-prd.md), the current parent implementation, and its proposed processors. This is an engineering applicability assessment for counsel and the launch owners, not a legal opinion, compliance certification, or evidence of executed vendor agreements. Recheck law, contracts and deployed configuration before real-family processing. Historical school-pilot approvals do not approve this new purpose.

## Decision for the current release

Keep real-family processing closed until a named privacy owner and counsel resolve the conditional roles below, approve the actual data/processor inventory and notices, and verify deletion and incident operations. The code contains useful controls; source code and synthetic tests do not establish that production operators, contracts, storage, backups and vendors satisfy those controls.

The most consequential unresolved classification is health information. Pregnancy/postpartum and WIC-related routing, linked to an account and combined with uploaded or partner information, deserve a specific FTC Health Breach Notification Rule assessment. Calling CaseLink a benefits-navigation app, or finding that HIPAA does not apply, does not answer that question.

## Actual data and responsibility boundary

The parent intake supports email authentication, needs/urgency, ZIP, language, household size, child age bands, pregnancy/postpartum, approximate income/frequency, benefit journey, utility provider, account authority and shutoff context. Bills/notices are optional private uploads with separate processing consent. Raw uploads can contain more identifiers than requested; a warning against uploading them does not remove that risk.

The current design sends permitted routing facts to Sol through Responses. The document pipeline first validates/parses files in an isolated worker, then sends selected financial/provider/date lines, rather than whole bills, to structured extraction. Source pages are privileged validation material. Neither raw pages nor extracted quotes belong in ordinary logs, analytics, notifications or provider packets. OpenAI input is still potentially personal data even after names are removed: combinations of household, reproductive, income and utility facts can remain linkable.

Relevant code is in `src/lib/parent/domain.ts`, `coordinator.ts`, `document-processing.ts`, `service.ts`, parent API routes, parent migrations, and `scripts/parent-support-retention.ts`. Review the deployed equivalents and vendor-side logs as well as these files. The document container has not been proven operational in this environment because the Docker daemon was unavailable; do not call the isolation deployment verified.

## Incident obligations need their own operational plan

For an ordinary entity under the PA Act, qualifying resident notice is without unreasonable delay after determination. More than 500 PA individuals triggers concurrent Attorney General notice; qualifying large incidents can also require consumer-reporting-agency notice and specified credit services. The Act contains different agency/contractor provisions; do not use one generic “72-hour” deadline for every situation. Counsel must decide the actual category and exceptions. The AG supplies a [breach reporting route](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/report-a-data-breach/); a prepared form is not a completed notice. [PA Act, §§3–5.4](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/US/HTM/2005/0/0094..HTM).

If HBNR applies, affected individuals and required media notices are due without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. FTC notice for 500 or more individuals is contemporaneous with individual notice; smaller incidents have an annual reporting rule. Media notice uses 500 or more residents in a state/jurisdiction. Service providers must notify the designated client and obtain acknowledgment. These are outside limits, not permission to wait. Preserve evidence and have counsel apply the exact discovery, content, delivery and exception provisions. [16 CFR 318.3–318.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-318).

The launch owner must name an incident lead, counsel contact and processor escalation contacts; rehearse containment, scoped evidence preservation, notification decisions and delivery. Never log raw document/model content to make incident investigation easier. An incident can involve unintended authorized-system disclosure, not only an outside attacker.

## OpenAI data-use and retention assessment

The reviewed code sets `store:false` on foreground Responses requests. OpenAI says API inputs/outputs are not used for training by default unless the customer opts in. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain content and remain for up to 30 days, with stated exceptions. `store:false` is not Zero Data Retention approval. ZDR/MAM require approval and actual organization/project settings; endpoint/features and notified model-specific safety-retention conditions still matter. Current documentation also describes encrypted prompt-cache application state for up to 24 hours. Files, background responses and third-party tools have separate behavior. HIPAA use requires an executed applicable healthcare addendum and eligible configured capabilities, not a code flag. [OpenAI data controls, reviewed October 25, 2026](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

Before approval, record the actual organization/project, model, endpoint and feature list; inspect opt-in and retention settings; obtain the relevant DPA/BAA and subprocessor/region terms; and resolve whether the approved retention is appropriate for the collected data. Do not send raw family bills to prove a configuration. Contract review and synthetic verification should precede family data. Keep minimized text-only extraction unless a separately reviewed capability requires more.

## Retention and public-notice reconciliation

The PRD values below are product defaults requiring a justified purpose and operational proof, not statutory periods prescribed for CaseLink by this review.

| Category | Current source-code behavior/requirement | Notice or evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unprocessed uploads | Initial expiry seven days. | Explain this is the CaseLink raw copy; expiry makes data eligible/inaccessible but is not proof a storage worker erased it. |
| Validated raw uploads | Foundation RPC shortens expiry to the earlier existing expiry or 24 hours after validation. Raw cleanup can preserve validated derived facts. | Explain the shorter post-extraction period and derived-fact distinction. Verify active handoff attachment needs are supported without silently retaining originals beyond the approved purpose. |
| Requests, household facts and immutable packets | Active service plus 90 days after closure/cancellation, or earlier eligible account deletion. Active/waiting requests do not simply expire90 days after creation. | Define closure, inactivity handling, parent deletion and any justified hold. Verify forms and partner copies separately. |
| Minimal retained evidence/tombstones | Defaults include 365-day expiry for selected hashes/evidence. | Approve necessity and fields; hashes are not automatically anonymous. Do not call this legally required without identifying the actual requirement. |
| Application/security logs and backups | PRD targets 30-day logs and backup expiry within 30 days of source deletion. | Verify hosting, monitoring, database/Auth/storage backups and restores. Source migrations do not establish vendor backup expiry or deletion reapplication. |
| Vendor and recipient copies | Outside the local cleanup transaction. | Publish feature-specific retention and recipient limits; obtain deletion/incident cooperation terms and explain limits on recalling delivered information. |

The early-access `/support/privacy` notice now distinguishes seven-day unprocessed raw expiry, at most 24 hours after validated extraction, retained derived facts, active-plus-90-day service records, the proposed 365-day minimal-evidence default, and vendor/backup copies. A named CaseLink privacy contact, approved processor terms, operational deletion evidence and final counsel-approved notice remain launch work. Do not replace those distinctions with blanket claims of zero retention or legally required audit periods.

The retention runner deletes private storage objects and Auth accounts before acknowledging eligible database cleanup. Evidence still needed: an isolated scheduled deployment, failure alerts, bounded cleanup delay, exact-target deletion tests, backup/restore rehearsal and retained-evidence inspection. The source reviewed here is `scripts/parent-support-retention.ts` and the parent foundation/form/facts migrations; record the release commit and deployed migration set when approving the schedule.
