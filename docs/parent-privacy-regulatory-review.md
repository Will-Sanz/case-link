## Incident obligations need their own operational plan

For an ordinary entity under the PA Act, qualifying resident notice is without unreasonable delay after determination. More than 500 PA individuals triggers concurrent Attorney General notice; qualifying large incidents can also require consumer-reporting-agency notice and specified credit services. The Act contains different agency/contractor provisions; do not use one generic “72-hour” deadline for every situation. Counsel must decide the actual category and exceptions. The AG supplies a [breach reporting route](https://www.attorneygeneral.gov/report-a-data-breach/); a prepared form is not a completed notice. [PA Act, §§3–5.4](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/WU01/LI/LI/US/HTM/2005/0/0094..HTM).

If HBNR applies, affected individuals and required media notices are due without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. FTC notice for 500 or more individuals is contemporaneous with individual notice; smaller incidents have an annual reporting rule. Media notice uses 500 or more residents in a state/jurisdiction. Service providers must notify the designated client and obtain acknowledgment. These are outside limits, not permission to wait. Preserve evidence and have counsel apply the exact discovery, content, delivery and exception provisions. [16 CFR 318.3–318.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-318).

## OpenAI data-use and retention assessment

The reviewed planning, extraction and assistant code sets `store:false` on foreground Responses requests. CaseLink's private background worker is distinct from choosing OpenAI's background-response feature; running after a page closes does not change the documented processor-retention controls. OpenAI says API inputs/outputs are not used for training by default unless the customer opts in. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain content and remain for up to 30 days, with stated exceptions. `store:false` is not Zero Data Retention approval. ZDR/MAM require approval and actual organization/project settings; endpoint/features and notified model-specific safety-retention conditions still matter. Current documentation also describes encrypted prompt-cache application state for up to 24 hours. Files, background responses and third-party tools have separate behavior. HIPAA use requires an executed applicable healthcare addendum and eligible configured capabilities, not a code flag. [OpenAI data controls, reviewed October 25, 2026](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

Before approval, record the actual organization/project, model, endpoint and feature list; inspect opt-in and retention settings; obtain the relevant DPA/BAA and subprocessor/region terms; and resolve whether the approved retention is appropriate for the collected data. Do not send raw family bills to prove a configuration. Contract review and synthetic verification should precede family data. Keep minimized text-only extraction unless a separately reviewed capability requires more.
