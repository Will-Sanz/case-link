## COMPASS: minimize effort without impersonation

There are two documented paths, not a compulsory CP account for each family. The [citizen quick reference guide](https://www.compass.dhs.pa.gov/assets/documents/pdf/CompassCitizenQRG.pdf), pages 7–9, permits continuing without an account. The [Community Partner guide](https://www.compass.dhs.pa.gov/assets/documents/pdf/CompassCPQRG.pdf), pages 16–17, also describes continuing a community-organization application without registering. A registered dashboard adds ongoing organization management; it is not evidence that unregistered help is prohibited. Current portal behavior and terms must still be verified before enabling browser execution.

A separate hidden-browser check opened the actual [public entry](https://www.compass.dhs.pa.gov/intake/#/getstarted) and its Apply Now control, which initialized the eight-stage application without login. It continued through tutorial/privacy, referral and WIC notices, the applicant-type and existing-account choices, and Family Safety. The citizen/family path continued after No to an existing account without requiring registration. The next provider-verification step returned a genuine inability-to-verify message; no CAPTCHA was solved or bypassed. No household values were entered and no application was submitted. The [live browser report](verification/parent-snap-browser-feasibility.md) records the precise stop and remaining work. CaseLink's new-application shortcut does not redirect renewal/pending/problem requests to a new application. This inspection does not establish later form fields, certification, final receipt or unattended execution; the current public privacy/terms page did not establish a general agent-specific permission or prohibition.

The CP guide, pages 10–12, allows delegated electronic signing when the head-of-household SSN is entered. It requires the applicant to understand rights/responsibilities and certify/agree to the declaration; the CP enters its own initials. It also states submitted information cannot be changed. Do not manufacture consent, type an invented person's signature, pretend the software is a human CP user, or repeatedly resubmit an uncertain response. Preserve assent, the exact submitted data and confirmation; route later corrections through the accepted process. The [DHS CP information page](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dhs/resources/for-dhs-partners/compass-partners) confirms organizations can initiate and track applications.

Application assistance, community-partner delegated signing, and formal authorized representation for interviews or ongoing reporting are distinct. [7 CFR 273.2(n)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/subtitle-B/chapter-II/subchapter-C/part-273/subpart-A/section-273.2) describes the application-processing representative as an adult sufficiently familiar with the household, designated in writing by a responsible household member. Software is not that adult. The regulation also protects immediate filing with the required minimum name/address/signature; do not hold up official filing while collecting optional materials. A [2023 DHS CP webinar](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dhs/documents/phe/documents/2023-01-24-QA-COMPASS-Community-Partner-QA.pdf) says a signed release is not required merely to submit applications/renewals through that CP assistance route. Its age and Medicaid focus mean it does not settle current SNAP representation or AI-automation terms.

AI can prepare an organization's registration using accurate supplied details. The [official CP registration guide](https://www.compass.dhs.pa.gov/assets/documents/pdf/CompassCPOnlineSelf-RegQRG.pdf) requires the organization FEIN and one to four named delegated administrators. An organization representative still needs to supply its real legal name, address, administrator/user identity and any required account verification or acceptance. Creating an organization identity or administrative authority from assumptions would not remove work; it would create false information and uncontrolled access. No current official source reviewed here grants a general API or unrestricted AI-robot authorization. Equally, absence of a public API does not prove a supported, permitted browser path cannot be built.

For the guest-browser route, the missing implementation is a reviewed household-isolated session handoff for genuine provider verification, protected direct identity entry, the remaining exact form/certification flow, permitted execution and reliable receipt/status recovery. An absent API or CP login does not by itself rule out that route. If an organization route is selected, establish which real legal organization operates it, any existing CP access, assigned administrators, current terms and required organization acceptance. AI can prepare routine setup from accurate supplied details; it cannot invent organizational identity or accept authority for an unnamed person. Credentials go through an approved secret connection, never family chat. Owner authorization to build does not replace any actual provider-required agreement.

## Sensitive identifiers

Do not collect an SSN in general intake, the family chat, ordinary facts, logs, analytics, notifications or model prompts. Current parent intake/packet schemas intentionally have no SSN field. Do not silently expand them because a future form might request it. Uploaded documents can contain identifiers even when the UI says not to upload them; parsing minimization and retention still apply.

The implemented assistant requires a processing notice, checks the current request owner/account and consent before model dispatch, minimizes context and rejects detected identifiers, account numbers, passwords and PIN patterns before persistence/model use. Detection is a guard, not proof that every possible secret or obfuscated identifier will be recognized. Do not turn chat into a required-application-field store. Request-scoped budgets and cancellation bound a question; normal support pause/cancellation does not silently restart application work when a parent asks about saved information.

For PECO and water, omit optional SSN/ITIN entirely. The [official 2026 PECO CAP form](https://www.peco.com/cdn/assets/v3/assets/blt1b5616c79bacadb4/blt9d4fc50508d00567/69f41a34626de91f304d9b8f/PECO_CAP_Application_2026_v2.pdf?branch=prod_alias) marks the field optional. The [City water application checklist](https://cap.phila.gov/) does the same. Do not require it as a CaseLink condition.

Where a verified SNAP route actually requires an applicant's SSN or electronic-signature identity value, prefer entry directly into the agency's secure page or an approved non-model credential/identifier channel. A fully delegated route would require a separately implemented and reviewed isolated identifier store and submitter: managed encryption keys separate from database credentials, exact household/purpose/recipient authorization, short retention, masked display, content-free auditing, audited access, no raw identifier in general packets/exports/backups/model context, deletion/restore controls, and processor/agency agreement. That identifier system is not implemented by this resource-directory change; collecting plaintext SSNs now would be unsafe. Never ask for EBT PINs or provider passwords in family chat.

The [FTC business security guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business) recommends collecting only legitimately needed sensitive information and keeping it only as long as needed. These engineering controls reduce exposure; no architecture or synthetic test establishes zero breach risk or legal compliance. The detailed conditional legal applicability, incident, health/WIC and contract questions remain in [the privacy review](parent-privacy-regulatory-review.md).
