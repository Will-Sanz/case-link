## Concrete proposed scope and retention

The optional purpose interprets at most 500 characters about immediate food and established SNAP journeys. The only extra typed facts sent are the three vetted current-tab routing answers, with their original confirmation/expiry dates. The private service may add a minimized summary of at most 20 current owned SNAP cases: need and recorded state. It strips account/request IDs and never reads raw family-profile, private application, identity, WIC/health, utility, document, or credential data for this purpose. Filtering is defense in depth; it cannot certify arbitrary prose safe.

The first server-created owned empty draft fixes both deadlines. For **24 hours**, content means prose, minimized model projection, validated structured selection, deterministic clarification, routing-source bindings, and content-derived hashes. Each charged revision retains its original minimized projection and validated selection within that same deadline. Reads, edits, clarification, retries, a new service request and sign-out do not renew it. Cancellation/account-purpose deletion clear this content immediately; a network failure is reported as unconfirmed cancellation rather than an erasure claim. An absolute UI deadline drops the local cache and invalidates late reads, including offline/BFCache resumption.

For **48 hours** from that same first creation, minimal owned evidence contains opaque draft/owner/revision/consent identifiers and dates, exact charge/project/response identifiers and complete-or-unknown token usage. This is a concrete review proposal, not approved policy. Anonymous service-wide call reservations contain no owner, draft, description, result or source hash and remain in the existing separate spending ledger; deleting or purging owned evidence never refunds a feature/global reservation. Processor and backup retention remain separate and must be verified before activation. An actual service request keeps an opaque description draft/revision pair and its original selected need IDs in the existing request-start audit under its ordinary service-record policy, without prose, a prose hash or a content-retention extension.

Each explicit queued revision has an original 15-minute private processing window. One reserved revision causes at most one Responses request; unknown charged delivery is not retried. A different key or display locale alone cannot charge the same prose/current minimized context again. A genuine explicit prose edit or changed trusted routing context can request a fresh revision only within the original 24-hour draft deadline and current gates. The resource ceiling of three charged interpretations/account/rolling 24 hours is a spending bound, not an evaluated quality threshold; all existing shared account/global limits also apply. Guided questions remain available.

The family-facing current-request handoff separately reads actual owned current scopes and prior uses of this exact source/revision. Those request IDs are never sent to the model. Covered needs open the actual request; an uncovered complementary food/SNAP need can start only with its own named mandate. One source/revision cannot start the same original need again after close, cancellation or later scope correction. A later disjoint need can start separately; neither link nor interpretation expands or resumes an earlier mandate.

## English candidate

### Optional: describe the food help you need

You can use the guided questions instead. Public food information and official program links remain available either way.

CaseLink sends your short description to OpenAI to suggest food or SNAP options and identify necessary routing questions. It uses only your current permitted routing answers and a limited summary of your own relevant food/SNAP requests: the kind of help and recorded progress, without names, account or request numbers, private documents or full case history. This version does not read your family profile or private applications.

Do not type names, contact details, street addresses, birth dates, Social Security or other identification numbers, case/account numbers, passwords, EBT PINs, signatures or document text. Do not include private WIC, pregnancy, child-health or utility-application information. A filter may miss sensitive information. Use the relevant protected form or official program for required private details.

This tool proposes options. It does not decide eligibility, reserve food, submit an application, contact a program or authorize service work. You still choose named programs and give a separate, bounded service mandate. Opening an existing owned request does not resume paused or cancelled work. Screen language does not change your service/contact language.

You choose when to send. Signing in does not send a description. Unsent text stays in this page's memory and may be lost if you reload or leave for account recovery. After an explicit send, the private service may finish that one interpretation after you close the page or sign out, within its original 15-minute permission window. You can cancel. An unconfirmed charged attempt is checked again, never automatically sent to OpenAI again.

CaseLink keeps description content, results, content-bearing routing sources and content-derived hashes for at most 24 hours from the first server-created draft. Reads, retries and edits do not extend that deadline. Cancellation clears that content immediately. Minimal consent, attempt and usage evidence lasts at most 48 hours from the same first creation. Deletion does not refund an already reserved call. An authorized support request separately keeps its non-content draft/revision reference and originally selected needs in its ordinary service audit history. Those references do not retain the description, its source content or its hash. Anonymous service-wide budget counts remain under the separate budget-ledger policy.

Cancelling cannot withdraw information OpenAI already received. OpenAI copies and backups have their own applicable settings and terms; CaseLink's 24-hour deadline does not promise those copies disappear then.

You can inspect retained information, download it, cancel an interpretation or request parent-purpose deletion even when new descriptions are closed. To stop service work you already authorized, use that request’s controls in My support. Parent-purpose deletion does not remove school records or the shared sign-in identity.

**Proposed choice:** “I choose this optional description tool and the processing explained here. I can use guided questions instead.”

## Texto propuesto en español

### Opcional: describa la ayuda alimentaria que necesita

Puede usar las preguntas guiadas. La información pública sobre alimentos y los enlaces oficiales siguen disponibles en ambos casos.

CaseLink envía su descripción breve a OpenAI para sugerir opciones de alimentos o SNAP e identificar las preguntas necesarias para orientarle. Solo usa sus respuestas actuales y permitidas para esa orientación y un resumen limitado de sus propias solicitudes pertinentes de alimentos o SNAP: el tipo de ayuda y el progreso registrado, sin nombres, números de cuenta o solicitud, documentos privados ni el historial completo del caso. Esta versión no lee su perfil familiar ni sus solicitudes privadas.

No escriba nombres, datos de contacto, domicilios, fechas de nacimiento, números de Seguro Social u otros números de identificación, números de caso o cuenta, contraseñas, PIN de EBT, firmas ni texto de documentos. No incluya información privada de WIC, del embarazo, de la salud de menores ni de solicitudes de servicios públicos. Un filtro puede no detectar información sensible. Use el formulario protegido correspondiente o el programa oficial para proporcionar los datos privados obligatorios.

Esta herramienta propone opciones. No determina si cumple los requisitos, reserva alimentos, presenta solicitudes, contacta programas ni autoriza trámites del servicio. Usted todavía elige los programas identificados y da un permiso separado y limitado para el servicio. Abrir una solicitud propia existente no reanuda el trabajo pausado o cancelado. El idioma de la pantalla no cambia su idioma para el servicio o los contactos.

Usted decide cuándo enviar. Iniciar sesión no envía una descripción. El texto no enviado permanece en la memoria de esta página y puede perderse al recargarla o salir para recuperar su cuenta. Después de un envío explícito, el servicio privado puede terminar esa única interpretación tras cerrar la página o la sesión, dentro de su plazo de permiso original de 15 minutos. Puede cancelarla. Un intento cobrado sin respuesta confirmada se consulta otra vez; nunca se vuelve a enviar automáticamente a OpenAI.

CaseLink conserva el contenido de la descripción, los resultados, las fuentes de orientación con contenido y las huellas digitales derivadas del contenido durante un máximo de 24 horas desde el primer borrador creado en el servidor. Leer, reintentar o editar no amplía ese plazo. Cancelar borra ese contenido inmediatamente. Las pruebas mínimas de consentimiento, intentos y uso se conservan durante un máximo de 48 horas desde la misma creación inicial. Eliminar no devuelve una llamada ya reservada. Una solicitud de apoyo autorizada conserva por separado la referencia sin contenido al borrador y su revisión, y las necesidades seleccionadas originalmente, en su historial ordinario del servicio. Esas referencias no conservan la descripción, el contenido de sus fuentes ni su huella digital. Los recuentos anónimos del presupuesto general del servicio permanecen según la política separada de ese registro.

Cancelar no retira la información que OpenAI ya haya recibido. Las copias de OpenAI y las copias de seguridad tienen sus propios ajustes y términos aplicables; el límite de 24 horas de CaseLink no promete que esas copias se borren entonces.

Puede consultar la información conservada, descargarla, cancelar una interpretación o solicitar la eliminación de los datos del servicio para padres, incluso cuando esté cerrada la recopilación de nuevas descripciones. Para detener trámites del servicio que ya autorizó, use los controles de esa solicitud en Mi apoyo. Esa eliminación no borra los registros escolares ni la identidad compartida para iniciar sesión.
