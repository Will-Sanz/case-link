## Defects found and addressed

- Intake and private application correction requests could wait indefinitely, including while reading a response body. They now use the existing bounded request helper, preserve uncertain attempts for the same-key retry, and ignore responses after a request change or unmount. Known SNAP mandate expiry closes further private collection.
- An ordinary household correction could overwrite editing done while an earlier save was pending. Native disabled controls and synchronous guards protect that draft. An owned routing correction remains possible after execution permission expires; that permission must not become an unnecessary restriction on editing one's own records.
- A generic program capability could offer a new submission for a SNAP renewal, pending application, benefit problem, or either WIC journey without a verified operation for that exact journey. Shared service/policy/UI checks and an unapplied SQL boundary now close those initial actions. Existing verified-reference follow-up is distinct.
- Private WIC questions were reachable at initial intake and return-use correction, and legacy health answers could reach the coordinator. New collection remains closed pending its assessment and permission. Existing records remain unchanged and readable; stored-record validation remains separate from fresh-input validation. Legacy private values are not silently cleared to make a correction succeed.
- Exact frozen SNAP submission and unknown-delivery reconciliation depended on fresh AI planning. A narrowly filtered continuation claim now protects unrelated queued jobs, and a deterministic SNAP pass precedes independent planning for other needs. The second pass excludes SNAP; its failure preserves recorded SNAP results and saved sibling work. A bounded five-minute retry lets other needs resume when their driver returns. Recovery cannot prepare or send another operation without that independent planning pass.

Each substantive fix has a runnable regression. The original defects and mixed-needs starvation were exercised before their fixes. Final normal review and a separate Ponytail pass are clear, including independent review of runtime `5c117a8ee844d4e2a4b529a5de8f633710d21f2fadd511e056c975dabf81f409` and its regression tests `8e1eb2569a5c775284895626a9653351223ebb419f489c8f57fb34077205fb9b`.
