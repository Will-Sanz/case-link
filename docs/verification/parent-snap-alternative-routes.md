## Delivery and status are separate proofs

The [DHS county directory](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dhs/contact/cao-information) lists Philadelphia headquarters and multiple district faxes. It does not establish a universal new-SNAP intake fax or a district mapping for ZIP 19104. Verify the exact destination and accepted packet before transmission; do not infer a recipient from proximity or use a LIHEAP-specific fax.

An API fax service is technically plausible. [Documo's API](https://docs.documo.com/) documents sending and status events; its [sandbox](https://help.documo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7789817420571-Sandbox-Environment) sends no real faxes but requires support-created access. No vendor account, credential, contract, sandbox access or transmission was created. This is a candidate, not the selected processor. Its [retention FAQ](https://help.documo.com/hc/en-us/articles/49189358689051-Common-FAQ) and [cold-storage policy](https://help.documo.com/hc/en-us/articles/25495625419803-Cold-Storage) require particular attention: attachment retention and indefinitely retained metadata are different. Review actual deletion/backups, content-bearing notifications, optional document AI, subprocessors and policy compatibility; use opaque metadata. HTTPS upload does not prove end-to-end encryption across the fax last mile.

Keep these states distinct: packet preparation; required personal certification; queued delivery; fax delivered/agency registration unconfirmed; agency registration; interview or evidence requested; decision; support received. A timeout or a blank status result must not trigger a duplicate new application. Reconcile the same immutable operation and bytes before retrying, including any fax vendor's own retries.

Pennsylvania's separate [application tracker](https://www.pa.gov/services/dhs/track-your-liheap-medicaid-snap-and-tanf-applications) publishes status, appointments and possible missing documents without a COMPASS account. Its [actual blank form](https://trackmybenefits.pa.gov/) was inspected in a hidden tab: household-head name and birth date; last four SSN digits or a COMPASS application number; and another matching county/contact/ZIP detail. The notice permits checking one's own application or another person's with permission. No lookup, checkbox acceptance or applicant entry occurred; no CAPTCHA was visible on the initial screen. Later verification, machine-access permission and successful lookup remain unproved. Fax filing might not produce a COMPASS number; a fax receipt or case number cannot be substituted. Last-four lookup requires its own protected, consented channel. No match is an unresolved state, not denial or proof of non-filing.
